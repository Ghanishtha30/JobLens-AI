Data Analyst (Fresher / 0-1 years)

About the role:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to turn business data into clear insights for our operations team.

Responsibilities:
- Clean and analyse datasets using Python and Pandas.
- Write SQL queries to extract data from our databases.
- Build dashboards in Power BI and share findings with stakeholders.
- Present insights to non-technical teammates with strong communication.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics or a related field.
- Python and SQL are required.
- Hands-on experience with Excel for reporting.
- Understanding of data visualization principles.
- Good problem solving and teamwork skills.

Nice to have:
- Tableau or Power BI experience.
- Knowledge of Machine Learning basics.
- Certification in data analytics is a plus.
